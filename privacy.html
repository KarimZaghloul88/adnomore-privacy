Privacy Policy for Adnomore + Earn Money Chrome Extension

Last Updated: April 14, 2025

Welcome to Adnomore + Earn Money (the "Extension"), provided by [Your Name/Company Name - REPLACE THIS] ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in relation to the Extension.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We prioritize your privacy. The Extension is designed NOT to collect or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). Specifically, we DO NOT collect:

  • Your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • Passwords, authentication credentials, or security questions.
  • Financial information like credit card numbers or bank details.
  • Health information.
  • Personal communications (emails, chats, etc.).
  • Detailed Browse history (the list of web pages you visit).
  • Keystrokes, precise mouse movements, or detailed scroll data.
  • Files from your computer.

2. Information We Handle

To provide its functionality, the Extension interacts with certain types of data locally on your device or through the integrated GetGrass service:

  • Ad Blocking Functionality:
    • Website Content Interaction: The Extension's content scripts analyze the structure (Document Object Model or DOM) of websites you visit solely to identify and block or hide elements determined to be advertisements or trackers. This interaction happens locally on your device. The content of the websites is not collected, stored, or transmitted by the Extension itself.
    • Network Request Blocking: The Extension uses Chrome's declarativeNetRequest API to block network requests matching predefined rules (e.g., requests to known ad servers). This prevents ads and trackers from loading without the Extension needing to read the content of the requests.
    • Aggregate Statistics: The Extension keeps a local count of the number of ads and trackers blocked, and estimates the data and time saved as a result. It may also locally store a list of the domains where the most ads were blocked. This information is stored only on your computer, is aggregated and non-identifiable, and is used solely to display performance statistics to you within the Extension's interface. It is never transmitted to us or any third party by the Extension.
  • Whitelisting:
    • If you choose to disable the Extension on specific websites (whitelist), the list of these website domains is stored locally on your computer within the Extension's settings. This allows the Extension to remember your preferences.
  • Extension Settings:
    • Settings such as whether the Extension is globally enabled or if dark mode is active are stored locally on your device.
  • GetGrass Integration:
    • The Extension facilitates connecting to the GetGrass service, allowing you to earn rewards by sharing unused internet bandwidth.
    • When you choose to use this feature and click the relevant links ("Earn Money Now", "Start Earning Now"), you are directed to the GetGrass website (app.getgrass.io).
    • Your interaction with the GetGrass service, including account creation, data sharing (like IP address and bandwidth usage required by their service), and rewards, is governed entirely by GetGrass's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We strongly recommend you review their policies:
      • GetGrass Privacy Policy: https://www.getgrass.io/privacy-policy
      • GetGrass Terms of Service: https://www.getgrass.io/terms-of-service
    • The Adnomore + Earn Money Extension does not receive, store, or process data you provide to GetGrass or data generated directly through your use of their service, other than potentially displaying basic status information if provided by GetGrass in the future.

3. Data Storage

All data handled by the Extension itself (settings, statistics, whitelist) is stored locally on your device using the browser's storage mechanisms (chrome.storage.local). We do not have access to this data.

4. Data Sharing and Transfer

We do not sell or transfer any user data collected or handled by the Extension itself to any third parties, except as required by law. Data shared with GetGrass is handled directly by them according to their policies.

5. Data Usage Purposes

Any data handled by the Extension is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Providing core ad and tracker blocking functionality.
  • Allowing user configuration (enabling/disabling, whitelisting, theme).
  • Displaying performance statistics (ads blocked, etc.) to the user locally.
  • Facilitating the connection to the optional GetGrass service.

We do not use this data for unrelated purposes, advertising, creditworthiness checks, or lending purposes.

6. Security

We implement reasonable measures within the Extension's code to help protect the information handled locally. However, no security system is impenetrable. Data related to the GetGrass service is secured by GetGrass according to their practices.

7. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or equivalent minimum age depending on jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
